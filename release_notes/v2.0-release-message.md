# v2.0 — Scientific correction: the single-domain no-go

A **major-version correction release**, not a polish pass. Paper IV of
the series — *Optical-Axis Anisotropy on the A=1 Discrete Causal
Lattice: Birefringence Cancellation and a Single-Domain No-Go*
([DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21435952](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21435952))
— establishes a **no-go result that materially corrects the substrate
this paper's derivation is carried out on**.

The finding, in one sentence: the original single-domain three-diagonal
hop set yields an **order-unity common-mode photon-speed anisotropy** —
an optical axis that is **observable but is not observed** — and is
therefore excluded as a physical vacuum construction.

**The A=1 conservation principle is not falsified.** The failure traces
to the hop-set *realization* — the omission of the fourth cube body
diagonal `V_4 = (1,1,-1)` — not to the conservation law. The lattice
geometry must change; the successor architecture is not birefringent.
The bump to 2.0 reflects that the derivation's substrate is
**superseded**, not merely annotated.

This is **Paper II of the A=1 Discrete Causal Lattice series**.

## What did NOT change

The paper body, appendices, figures, notes, and verification scripts
are unchanged. They document the reasoning that led to the falsifying
result and are deliberately not rewritten. Where the revision notice
qualifies a body claim, **the notice is authoritative**.

The **algebraic results stand**: containment `⊇`, the dimension-71
centralizer `su(6) ⊕ su(6) ⊕ u(1)`, and the factor-product projection
do not depend on the hop set's phenomenological viability.

Results that *do* depend on the specific gauge-sector realization are
to be read conditionally — the birefringence corollary among them,
since it **is** the excluded anisotropy. The `g_3^2 / g_2^2 = 3/2`
ratio is **left open**: the Q-tensor and the universal prefactor cancel
in the ratio, so it may survive the no-go, but the successor geometry
could change the spectator counting it rests on.
