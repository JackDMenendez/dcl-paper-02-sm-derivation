Zenodo "References" field for Paper II v2.0
=============================================

Paste the five lines below "BEGIN REFERENCES" into Zenodo's
"References" field (one citation per line).  This is the field
that records the bibliographic references the deposit cites, in
plain-text citation-string form.  It is separate from the
"Related/alternate identifiers" field (see
zenodo_related_works.txt for that one).

If you prefer to paste rich text, zenodo_references.html in this
directory carries the same five citations as an HTML ordered list
with live DOI links.  Zenodo's rich-text fields accept that markup
directly; the plain-text block below remains the fallback.

Order matches the paper's BibTeX bibliography (unsrt style:
numeric, in citation order) as emitted by the v2.0 build.

------------------------ BEGIN REFERENCES ------------------------

Notes:

- NEW IN v2.0: Paper IV (Optical-Axis Anisotropy, doi:10.5281/
  zenodo.21435952) is now the FIRST reference.  It is cited on
  page 1, in the front-matter revision notice that precedes the
  abstract, and it is the source of the single-domain no-go that
  this version records.  It is also added to "Related identifiers"
  with Relation = "Is supplemented by" / "References" -- see
  zenodo_related_works.txt.

- CITATION ORDER CHANGED at v2.0.  Because unsrt numbers entries
  in order of first citation and the revision notice sits ahead of
  the abstract, Paper IV takes position 1 and everything else
  shifts down.  Georgi and Sakharov also swapped relative to v1.0.
  The authority is the generated bibliography
  (.build/dcl-paper-02-sm-derivation.bbl), not this file -- if the
  two disagree after a rebuild, the .bbl wins and this file should
  be regenerated from it.

- Sakharov 1968 is recorded here in citation-string form (where
  the reprint DOI in brackets is informative but not required),
  even though it is intentionally omitted from the "Related
  identifiers" field (where every entry should have a clean
  resolvable identifier on its own).  This keeps Zenodo's
  human-readable reference list aligned with the paper's
  bibliography while keeping the machine-readable citation graph
  clean.

- The Paper I citation here is the same Zenodo deposit that is
  ALSO recorded under "Related identifiers" with Relation =
  Continues.  That dual presence is correct: "References" tracks
  what the deposit cites; "Related identifiers" tracks the
  citation-graph relations Zenodo uses for navigation.  Tooling
  that walks one path or the other should find Paper I in both.
